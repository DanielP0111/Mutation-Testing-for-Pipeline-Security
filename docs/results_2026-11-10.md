# Benchmark run on git with the per-phase sandbox (2026-11-10)

Run: `data/benchmark_nov10` (20 mutants × 1 run, `--reference-placement`), log
`data/benchmark_nov10.log`. Baseline `data/evaluation/baseline_foreman` (built
under Foreman); plain baseline `data/evaluation/baseline_full` reproduced twice
with identical hashes. Harness as on Oct 30 (release-style tree, wraps
re-extracted every build, `--wrap-mode forcefallback`), plus the step.sh fix that
builds generated headers before `meson compile`.

Defenses: `wrap_integrity_check` (static, blocking), Foreman with a file-level
policy (dynamic, detecting), and our per-phase sandbox (dynamic, enforcing; see
docs/defenses.md).

## Validity

Same as Oct 30: effective 5 (op_005, op_013, op_014, op_015, op_016),
build_valid 11, setup_failed 4, flaky 0. The Oct 30 result reproduces.

## False positives on a clean rebuild

Foreman (learned file-level policy): 0 warnings. Sandbox (learned policy: fetch
may create entries in `subprojects/`; setup, compile and test may write only
`build/`): clean build passes all 4 tests, 0 false positives.

## Findings

1. **The sandbox and Foreman fail in opposite places.** Foreman misses the two
   `/tmp` payloads (outside the tree it watches); the sandbox confines exactly
   those, because each phase's `/tmp` is discarded. The in-tree payloads that
   Foreman detects are the ones the sandbox prevents outright (read-only source).
2. **Blocked is mostly confined, not prevented.** 3 of 5 payloads still RAN
   under the sandbox; their effect just did not outlive the phase. op_014 shows
   the difference: the sandbox does not stop the fixture read Foreman flags (the
   optional "hide files a phase never read" step would).
3. **Not stressed yet: no effective mutant targets `build/`, the network, or an
   artifact.** All 5 effects are marker files, so not_blocked = 0 says nothing
   about the known `build/` gap or about exfiltration. The test-phase operators
   (Debora) and the /tmp vs build/ split placement (David) are what test that.

Note: the sandbox also records an outcome for non-effective mutants (op_012 shows
"prevented" because its effect never happens even without a defense). Only
effective mutants are scored.
